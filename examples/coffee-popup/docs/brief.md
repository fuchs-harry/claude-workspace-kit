# Brief: Coffee Pop-up Weekend

## In one sentence
Two days at the Saturday market to test if people want our coffee as a monthly subscription.

## Why it matters
Before we invest in a roaster and a webshop, we want real customers telling us (with their email address) that they'd subscribe.

## What we know already
- Jonas can give us a 3 × 3 m spot at the market for 80 € per day.
- Mia's espresso blend gets great feedback from friends.
- We have about 900 € budget in total.

## What we still need to find out
- Which weekend (see call notes).
- Price per cup.
- Whether we can take card payments.
